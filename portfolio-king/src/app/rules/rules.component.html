Rules & Regulations

A screening for the final teams (in case of a greater number of participants) will be done beforehand and they will be informed accordingly.

Proposed number of rounds: Eight (8)

Maximum Number of teams playing at the same time: Four (4)

Members per team must only be Three (3)

Noncompliance of rules will be a disqualification.

The decision of the panel of Judges will be final and binding on all concerned.

Use of mobile phones is prohibited and will lead to disqualification automatically.

Proper discipline and decorum has to be maintained.

Time allowed for each team is time taken for throwing of Dice- 30 seconds
