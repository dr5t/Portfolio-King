Article I

Cohort Eligibility & Participation Structure

Prior to tournament commencement, a formal participant screening process is conducted. Only verified syndicates are provisioned credentials by the Executive Game Administrator.

  • A maximum of four (4) institutional syndicates compete simultaneously per trading session.
  • Each syndicate must authenticate with their assigned unique Team ID and password.
  • No substitute credentials or unregistered participant additions are permitted once rounds have started.
Article II

Market Cycles & Execution Protocol

The simulation models 8 successive quarterly market periods across 10 asset classes, debt instruments, and Fund-of-Funds allocations.

  • Decision Latency: Each syndicate is allocated an exact 30-second window to roll market event dice and commit capital.
  • Asset Allocation: Capital deployed into Bank FD, Corporate Bonds, Equities, Real Estate, and Gold ETF must adhere to risk limits and available cash.
  • Automatic Execution: If the decision timer expires before manual commitment, standard market fallback logic executes for the active period.
Article III

Institutional Governance & Decorum

To preserve market integrity, strict competition standards are enforced by presiding simulation judges.

  • Device Prohibition: Unauthorized external financial software or mobile devices are strictly prohibited during live trading rounds.
  • Disqualification: Noncompliance with administrative rulings or unsportsmanlike conduct results in immediate syndicate disqualification.
  • Judicial Finality: Determinations made by the presiding Executive Panel of Judges are final, conclusive, and binding upon all participants.
